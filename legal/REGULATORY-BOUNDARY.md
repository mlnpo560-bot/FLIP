# FLIP Regulatory Boundary

FLIP is an open-source protocol, not a declaration of legal tender.

The protocol can be distributed internationally, but each jurisdiction determines whether virtual-asset activities, payment services, custody, exchange, issuance or related businesses are permitted and what authorization or compliance is required.

FATF's current standards call for jurisdictions to regulate and supervise virtual-asset service providers, including licensing or registration and AML/CFT measures. citeturn0search0turn0search1

EU deployment must account for the Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) framework and its implementing measures. citeturn0search2turn0search3

Therefore FLIP's implementation target is:

**globally distributable protocol + jurisdiction-aware interfaces + compliance-ready service layer.**

No repository document should state that FLIP itself is automatically legal worldwide.
